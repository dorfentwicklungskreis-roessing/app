The app now opens on a home screen with sections. "Helping out" comes
first — formerly "Village care". Map, list and leaderboard now live
together in that section, and the tile shows how many spots are
waiting for you.

"My profile" is new: a display name plus, entirely optional, phone,
e-mail and a note. Every field has its own switch — only what you
release is visible to others.

"Villagers" shows you who else takes part.

The whole look has been freshened up.
