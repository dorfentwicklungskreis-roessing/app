The village app for Rössing (municipality of Nordstemmen, Germany). The app is in German only.

IMPORTANT BEFORE INSTALLING
You can only use this app with a Rössing-ID, the village account operated at id.rössing.de. Without such an account you cannot get past the sign-in screen.

WHAT THE APP DOES
- Map of all flower boxes, beds and other places the village looks after, each with a traffic-light status (green = fine, yellow = due, red = overdue)
- Optional: with location permission granted, the map shows your own position and the distance to each place, and the list can be sorted by proximity. The position never leaves the device.
- Watering and weeding schedules with intervals; a heat factor can make watering tasks fall due sooner
- Report a completed task in two taps, with a confirmation step; reports can be withdrawn again. A task that was just done stays locked for a while, so the same box cannot be "watered" three times in a row
- Leaderboard per week, month, watering season, year or all time, plus the village totals

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
No ads, no tracking, no analytics SDKs. No in-app purchases. No background location and no transmission of your position.

DATA
Name and e-mail address come from the Rössing-ID at sign-in. The village server stores only the task reports: user id, display name, timestamp, litres and an optional note. Reports are visible to all signed-in villagers. All traffic uses HTTPS. Map tiles come from OpenFreeMap (OpenStreetMap data), which therefore sees the device IP address.

The servers and accounts are run by the Dorfentwicklungskreis Rössing itself. There is no company behind it and nothing is being sold.
