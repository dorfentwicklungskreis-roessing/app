Test build of the Rössing village app.

The app is meant for the residents of Rössing (municipality of Nordstemmen, Germany). It shows what needs doing in the village: flower boxes and beds on a map, who watered or weeded last, the village calendar and a leaderboard as encouragement. The interface is in German only.

SIGN-IN REQUIRED
Without a Rössing-ID you cannot get past the sign-in screen. Accounts are issued by the Dorfentwicklungskreis.

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING AT
- Signing in and out: does the browser return to the app cleanly?
- Map: do the tiles load, is the initial region right, are the pins in place?
- Reporting a completed task and withdrawing it again.
- Profile: are the per-field visibility switches understandable?
- Layout on different device sizes, in light and dark appearance.
